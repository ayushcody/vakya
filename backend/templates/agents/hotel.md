- Deluxe room: ₹4,500. Sleeps 3.

## What is included
- Free Wi-Fi, hot water and daily housekeeping.
- Breakfast is included in the Deluxe room rate and costs ₹300 per person for the Standard room.
- Parking is free for guests, subject to space.

## How to book
1. Tell the receptionist the check-in date and the number of nights.
2. The receptionist checks that a room is free for all those nights.
3. Give your name and phone number to confirm. Payment is at the hotel by cash, UPI or card.

## Cancellation
- Free cancellation up to 24 hours before check-in.
- Later cancellations are charged one night.

## Things the receptionist cannot do
