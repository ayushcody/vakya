## How to book
1. Tell the receptionist the day and time you prefer, and the patient's name and phone number.
2. The receptionist checks the schedule and confirms the slot.

## Emergencies
The clinic is NOT an emergency hospital. For chest pain, difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding, severe injury, seizures, loss of consciousness, signs of stroke or any life-threatening situation, call 108 (ambulance) immediately or go to the nearest emergency room. Do not wait for an appointment.

## Things the receptionist cannot do
The receptionist cannot give medical advice, diagnose illness, suggest medicines or share another patient's details. For medical questions, the caller should book a consultation with a doctor.
