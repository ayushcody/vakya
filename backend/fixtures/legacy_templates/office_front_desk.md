# Joshi & Bhosale Associates - Front Desk Guide

Sample data for a fictional organisation.

## About the office
Joshi & Bhosale Associates is a small chartered accountancy and legal practice at Office 304, Gangapur Road, Nashik 422013. The front desk phone number is 0253-5550-0188. The office is closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

## Office hours
- Monday to Friday: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with a lunch break from 1:30 PM to 2:30 PM.
- Saturday: 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM.
- During the weeks around 31 July and 31 March the office stays open until 8:00 PM on weekdays. The front desk will say if the timings change.

## Who handles what, and appointment days
- CA Suresh Joshi, Chartered Accountant (income tax, audit and company accounts): Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM.
- CA Pooja Kulkarni, GST and compliance (GST registration, monthly returns, notices): Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 11:00 AM to 1:30 PM and 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM.
- Adv. Neha Bhosale, Advocate (agreements, property documents, legal notices, company registration): Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM, plus Tuesday and Thursday 3:00 PM to 5:30 PM.

## Fees
- Consultation: ₹500 for 30 minutes with a chartered accountant, and ₹800 for 30 minutes with the advocate. The consultation fee is adjusted against the work fee if the caller engages the office within 7 days.
- Income tax return for a salaried person: ₹1,500. For a small business or professional with books: from ₹3,500, depending on the work.
- GST registration: ₹2,500. Monthly GST return filing: ₹800 per month for up to 50 invoices.
- Rent agreement drafting: ₹2,000 plus stamp duty and registration charges as per government rates.
- Company or LLP registration: from ₹9,000 plus government fees. The exact quote is given after the first meeting.
- The office does not quote fees for notices, appeals or court matters over the phone. These are decided by the partner after reading the papers.
- Payment is accepted by UPI, bank transfer and cheque. Cash receipts are given for amounts up to ₹10,000 only.

## Documents to bring
- Income tax return, salaried: Form 16, the bank statement for the year, the interest certificates, and proof of investments for deductions. Originals are not kept; photocopies or clear photos are enough.
- Income tax return, business or professional: the books or sales and purchase records, the bank statements, and last year's return.
- GST registration: proof of the business address such as a rent agreement or electricity bill, the owner's photographs, and the bank account details. ID and address proof are checked in person at the office; please do not read ID numbers out over the phone.
- GST notice: the notice itself, the last three months of returns, and the reply if one was already sent.
- Rent agreement: ID proof of both parties, the property address, the rent and deposit amounts, and the agreement period.
- For any new client, a copy of last year's return and the details of the previous accountant are helpful but not required.

## How to book an appointment
1. Call the front desk on 0253-5550-0188 between 10:00 AM and 5:30 PM on weekdays, or between 10:00 AM and 1:30 PM on Saturday.
2. Tell us which partner or which work you need (income tax, audit, GST or legal), the preferred day and time, and your name and phone number.
3. We confirm the slot by SMS. Please arrive 10 minutes early and bring the documents listed above for your work.
4. To cancel or reschedule, please inform us at least 2 hours before the appointment. There is no cancellation charge.
- Walk-in visitors are met only if the partner is free. It is better to book.
- Appointments are 30 minutes. For longer work, such as company registration, the partner will schedule a second meeting.

## Important dates
- Income tax return filing for individuals: 31 July, unless the government extends it.
- Advance tax instalments: 15 June, 15 September, 15 December and 15 March.
- GST monthly returns: the 11th and the 20th of every month for most small businesses.
- The front desk can remind callers of these dates but cannot say whether an extension has been announced; for that, book a call with the partner.

## Things the front desk cannot do
The front desk cannot give tax or legal advice, say whether a notice is serious, predict the result of a case, quote a fee that is not listed here, or share another client's details. For such questions, the caller should book a consultation with the right partner. The front desk never asks for PAN, Aadhaar or bank passwords on the phone.
