# Pune Institute of Technology - Admissions Desk Guide

Sample data for a fictional organisation.

## About the institute
Pune Institute of Technology (PIT) is a fictional engineering and management college located at Survey No. 45, Hinjewadi Phase 2, Pune 411057. Admissions desk phone: 020-5550-0377, open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Email: admissions@pit-sample.edu.

## Programmes and intake
- B.Tech Computer Engineering: 120 seats.
- B.Tech Information Technology: 60 seats.
- B.Tech Electronics and Telecommunication: 60 seats.
- B.Tech Mechanical Engineering: 60 seats.
- MBA (2 years): 60 seats.

## Eligibility
- B.Tech: 12th pass (Science) with Physics and Mathematics plus Chemistry, Biology or a technical subject, with at least 50 percent marks overall (45 percent for reserved categories). A valid MHT-CET or JEE Main score is required.
- MBA: any graduate with at least 50 percent marks (45 percent for reserved categories) and a valid CAT, MAH-CET or CMAT score.

## Key dates for the 2026-27 intake
- Online application opens: 10 May 2026.
- Last date to apply: 30 June 2026.
- Merit list published: 8 July 2026.
- Document verification: 10 to 14 July 2026.
- Fee payment to confirm the seat: by 18 July 2026.
- Classes begin: 3 August 2026.

## Fees per year (tuition)
- B.Tech (all branches): ₹1,45,000 per year. For reserved categories eligible under the government scheme: ₹24,000 per year.
- MBA: ₹1,60,000 per year.
- Application fee: ₹800 (₹400 for reserved categories). Non-refundable.
- Exam and library fee: ₹6,000 per year.

## Documents required
Class 10 and 12 marksheets, entrance exam score card, school leaving certificate, Aadhaar card, caste and non-creamy-layer certificates (if applicable), domicile certificate, 6 passport photos, and migration certificate (for students from other boards).

## Hostel
- Separate hostels for boys (capacity 300) and girls (capacity 200), inside the campus.
- Hostel fee: ₹85,000 per year including mess (vegetarian and non-vegetarian options). Security deposit: ₹10,000, refundable.
- Hostel application is made together with the admission form. Hostel seats are given on a first-come basis after seat confirmation.

## Scholarships
Merit scholarship: students scoring above 95 percentile in MHT-CET receive a 25 percent tuition waiver in the first year.

## Note
The admissions desk cannot guarantee a seat or a merit rank. Final admission depends on the merit list and document verification.
