# Sunrise Family Clinic - Reception Guide

Sample data for a fictional organisation.

## About the clinic
Sunrise Family Clinic is a small family clinic at 14 Lake View Road, Shivajinagar, Pune 411005. The reception phone number is 020-5550-0142. The clinic is closed on public holidays.

## Timings
- Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, with a lunch break from 1:30 PM to 3:00 PM.
- Sunday: 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM, for emergency walk-in consultations only.
- The pharmacy inside the clinic is open from 9:00 AM to 7:30 PM, Monday to Saturday.

## Consultation fees
- General Physician: ₹400 for a first visit and ₹250 for a follow-up within 7 days.
- Paediatrician: ₹500 for a first visit and ₹300 for a follow-up within 7 days.
- Gynaecologist: ₹700 for a first visit and ₹400 for a follow-up.
- Dentist: ₹300 consultation. Treatments are charged separately after the examination.
- Dermatologist: ₹600 for a first visit and ₹350 for a follow-up.
- Basic blood tests, such as sugar and haemoglobin, are done at the in-house lab. Reports are ready the same day by 6:00 PM for samples given before 12:00 noon.

## How to book an appointment
1. Call reception on 020-5550-0142 between 9:00 AM and 7:30 PM, or send a WhatsApp message to 98900 55501.
2. Tell us the doctor you want, your preferred day and time, and the patient's name and age.
3. We confirm the slot by SMS. Please arrive 10 minutes early and carry any old reports or prescriptions.
4. To cancel or reschedule, please inform us at least 3 hours before the appointment. There is no cancellation charge.
- Walk-in patients are seen after booked patients. Waiting time is usually 20 to 30 minutes.
- Payment is accepted by cash, UPI and debit or credit card.

## Emergencies
The clinic is NOT an emergency hospital. For chest pain, difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding, severe injury, seizures, loss of consciousness, signs of stroke, or any life-threatening situation, call 108 (ambulance) immediately or go to the nearest emergency room. Do not wait for an appointment at the clinic. The nearest hospital with a 24-hour emergency room is Lakeside General Hospital, 2 km from the clinic.

## Things the receptionist cannot do
The receptionist cannot give medical advice, diagnose illness, suggest medicines or share another patient's details. For medical questions, the caller should book a consultation with a doctor.
