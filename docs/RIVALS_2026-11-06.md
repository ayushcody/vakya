## The rules that matter

- Deadline 10 Nov 2026, 11:59 PM IST. Demo video posted on LinkedIn and/or X with #GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge and
  #GnaniAI, tag Gnani AI; one nominated post per platform; verified reactions, comments and reshares decide the shortlist.

  use), Small Town Big Build (Tier 2/3), **Demo Day Drop (best 60-second video)**, Crowd Favourite (public votes).

## What this means for Vakya

- **Almost nobody uses a real phone line.** The entries run in a browser microphone or on recordings, and the best ones say so
  ("simulated call", "Android does not let an app listen"). Vakya answers a real number over 8 kHz telephony. Lead with that:
  call it on camera.
- **Noise Canceller is the award to aim for** (with Real Deal as the second fit). Scam Sentinel already publishes noise
  numbers (13 dB and 5 dB), so Vakya needs its own: task completion on clean, 8 kHz, noisy and codec audio.
- **relay.voice is the direct rival** (team of two, booking, reminders, dashboard, 60-second demo). Vakya's differences to
  show: caller memory ("Welcome back Zoran, your appointment is tomorrow at 3 PM"), WhatsApp confirmations, Google Calendar,
  eight trades on one platform, Hindi and Marathi with code-mixing, consent and receipts.
- **Engagement is low everywhere** (the best seen is 42 reactions). A good post out early can lead. The posts that do well open
  with a one-line story, show a working demo, give one honest limitation and end with a question.
